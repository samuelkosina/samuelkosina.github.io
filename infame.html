Light for the INFAME RADIO.
005 | 2026


Infame Radio is a community radio platform based in Brno, CZ, located within Vitamin — a community center currently in development and connected to a café. This ongoing project began with the design of a light for the radio booth and gradually expanded into a broader lighting concept for the entire space. Currently, we are developing prototype lamps that will be introduced as stage design during the Infame Radio festival in March 2026. The project continues toward finalizing the lighting objects for their permanent placement within Vitamin. The project is developed in collaboration with Jovana Kalajdžić.
