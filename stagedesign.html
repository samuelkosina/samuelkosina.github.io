Scenic Design
005 | 2024-NOW

authors of the photos and videos — Me and Richard Steinbach

I work on stage designs and site-specific scenic installations for cultural events and performances. The projects often involve reusing and transforming materials, giving them a new temporary purpose within each installation.


An important part of the work is lighting — operating and programming DMX-controlled systems, building custom lights, and developing live-rendered visuals. I also work with projection mapping and live projections on buildings.


I regularly collaborate as a member of several Brno-based collectives and event productions.
